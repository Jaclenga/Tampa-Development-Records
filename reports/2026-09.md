# Tampa Published Development Records — September 2026 update

Comparison: **2026-08-23 → 2026-09-01**

## What changed in the published layers

- 183 newly observed source records
- 951 records no longer returned
- 113 status changes
- 22 newly observed planning applications
- 27 capital-project phase changes
- 310 estimated-cost changes
- 40 planned-date changes

The earlier snapshot contained 4,469 source records; the later snapshot contained 3,701. A total of 2,040 record identities had at least one reported change.

## Interpretation

This report compares two snapshots of eight named City of Tampa public GIS layers. A newly observed record may have existed before the interval, and a record that is no longer returned is not necessarily deleted, cancelled, or complete. Permit issuance is authorization, planned dates are schedules, and estimated costs are not actual spending.
